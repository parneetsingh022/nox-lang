# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- Being respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Giving and accepting technical feedback in good faith.
- Focusing criticism on ideas and code rather than individuals.
- Respecting differing viewpoints, experiences, and levels of expertise.
- Acknowledging mistakes and working to resolve disagreements productively.
- Helping create an environment where people feel safe asking questions and contributing.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Insulting, demeaning, or inflammatory comments.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or sustained bad-faith arguments.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Threats of violence or encouraging harm toward others.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, code reviews, chat channels, community events, and other public or private communication related to the project.

It also applies when someone is representing the project in an official capacity.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainers.

Until a dedicated contact address is published, reports may be sent through a private contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue.

Reports should include, when possible:

- A description of what happened.
- Where and when it happened.
- Relevant links, screenshots, or other context.
- Whether the behavior is ongoing.
- Any immediate safety concerns.

All reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as reasonably possible.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate it.

Depending on the severity and circumstances, enforcement actions may include:

1. A private warning.
2. Removal of inappropriate content.
3. Temporary restrictions from project participation.
4. Permanent removal from project spaces.
5. Escalation to the relevant platform or authorities when necessary.

Enforcement decisions will be made in good faith with the goal of protecting the community.
